Curioteca Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Curioteca is an online shop that sells printed products through Etsy at curioteca.etsy.com. This policy covers the Curioteca Pin Scheduler, a tool the shop uses to publish pins to its own Pinterest account.

What the tool does

The Curioteca Pin Scheduler signs in to the shop's own Pinterest account through Pinterest's official login. It creates pins showing the shop's products, each linking to the product's Etsy listing, and reads the shop's own boards so each pin is placed on the correct board.

Information we access

The tool accesses only the Curioteca Pinterest account: its account name, its boards, and the pins it creates. It does not access the accounts, boards, pins, or personal information of any other Pinterest user.

Information we store

The tool stores the access token Pinterest issues for the shop's account, the ID of each pin it creates, and the date it was posted. This information is kept privately by the shop owner and is used only to run the shop's Pinterest account.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any information obtained through Pinterest with anyone.

Purchases

Orders are placed and handled on Etsy. Information you give when buying is governed by Etsy's Privacy Policy.

Deleting data

The shop can revoke the tool's access at any time from Pinterest's settings, after which the stored token no longer works and is deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to curiotecashop@gmail.com.
